I finalized what I want perks to be how they should act. Perks will be things that a single ship can pick up that modify the actions of the ship or that team. 
Perks will be icons in the sea that ships that acquire by moving through.
The ship that moves through the perk acquires that perk.
If that ship dies, then the perk drops where that ship was located.

THe perk names and what they do:

1. The black pearl. This ship has a 7.5% chance to convert an enemy ship that is killed in combat against this ship to its own team. It joins the team in the place that it was killed. It does not count toward dice count in the round it was converted. 
2. Glass Cannon. This ship rolls an eight sided die.
3. Loaded Dice. If this ship were to roll a 1 or a 2, it instead rolls a 3.
4. The architect. If this ship is stationed in the blue shallow waters of an ally port, ships build one day quicker.
